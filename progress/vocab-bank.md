# Vocabulary bank

Built by `/vocab` ADD, tested by `/vocab` QUIZ, exported by `/anki`.

**The collocation column is the important one.** Knowing a word and knowing how
to use it are different skills, and IELTS Lexical Resource tests the second.
*Make research* and *conduct research* differ by one word and one band.

**Anti-rules:** no forced idioms in Writing (they belong in Speaking); no
thesaurus substitution — band 7 is the *right* word, not the rare one.

**Testing order:** never-tested first, then oldest last-tested date, then items
previously wrong.
