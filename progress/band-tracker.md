# Band tracker

Every graded task. `/review` reads this weekly for trends.

**Target: 7.0 overall.** Minimum acceptable for admission: 6.0–6.5.

**Type** is one of: `drill` (a normal `/writing1`, `/writing2`, `/speaking`,
`/reading`, `/listening` session) · `MOCK` (a full timed `/mock`) · `OFFICIAL`
(official Cambridge or ielts.org material processed through `/official`).
