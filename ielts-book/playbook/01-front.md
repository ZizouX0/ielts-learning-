## If you do only five things

**1. Fix your articles.**
*the society* → society. But *the government* keeps its *the*.
→ **The single biggest difference between band 6 and band 7 writers with your
first languages.**

**2. Answer every part of the Task 2 question.**
Two views asked for? Give both, plus your opinion, with equal weight.
→ **Miss one part and you are capped at band 5 on Task Response.**

**3. Develop every idea: claim → because → so what → example.**
Never state a point and move on.
→ **This is what separates band 6 from band 7 more than anything else.**

**4. On computer Reading, copy-paste the answer from the passage.**
Never type it. Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V.
→ **Makes spelling errors impossible on completion questions.**

**5. Slow down when you speak.**
Speaking fast to sound fluent is a band-6 marker.
→ **Fluency means not stopping, not speed.**

## The 60-second proofread — run this on every piece of writing

Last three minutes. In this order. Most expensive first.

1. **Articles** — every abstract noun. *the society* ✗ · *the government* ✓
2. **Words ending in -s** — is it uncountable? *informations* ✗ *researches* ✗
3. **Third-person -s** — he *work* ✗ → he *works* ✓
4. **Long sentences** — over 25 words? Find where it should have ended.
5. **Word count** — on screen, bottom left. Subtract anything you copied.

**Delete and repair. Never add.** New sentences written in a panic carry new errors.
