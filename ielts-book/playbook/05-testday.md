# TEST DAY — the running order

---

## LISTENING · ~30 min

**Before each part:** read the questions. For every gap decide — number? name?
plural? Write nothing yet.

**While it plays:** type as you hear. Never wait.

**The three rules:**
- One answer per box. Two scores zero.
- Never a blank. Guess something of the right type.
- Answers come in order. Missed one? Go forward. Never hunt backwards.

**At the end:** 2 minutes. Check spelling and plurals only. Do not change answers.

---

## READING · 60 min

**Stop at 20 minutes per passage.** Even mid-question. Even if it's nearly solved.

**Order:** Matching Headings early. Matching Information last.

**Copy-paste from the passage** for every completion answer. Ctrl+F does not work.

**True / False / Not Given — one question:**
> *Can I point at the sentence that contradicts this?*
> **Yes → FALSE. No → NOT GIVEN.**

Never a blank.

---

## WRITING · 60 min

**If you run out of time on tests, do Task 2 first. It is worth double.**

### Task 1 — 20 min
```
0–5    Read. Spot the biggest pattern. Decide your two groups.
5–17   Intro (1 sentence) → Overview (2) → Body 1 → Body 2
17     STOP WRITING
17–20  Check
```
No conclusion. No opinions. No causes.

### Task 2 — 40 min
```
0–5    Underline EVERY part of the question. Plan 2 ideas.
5–37   Intro → Body 1 → Body 2 → Conclusion
37     STOP WRITING
37–40  The 60-second proofread
```
260–290 words. Longer is fine if it's accurate.

---

## SPEAKING · 11–14 min

**Part 1** — Answer + reason + example. Two or three sentences. Never one word.

**Part 2** — The prep minute is for **keywords, not sentences.**
Speak the full two minutes. **Being stopped means you spoke long enough.**

**Part 3** — Expect pushback. It is not hostility, it is the test.
**Concede, then hold your ground with a reason.**

Don't know a word? Paraphrase it. That scores band 7, not zero.

---

## When you get stuck

| Paper | Do this |
|---|---|
| Listening | Guess. Move forward. Never chase backwards. |
| Reading | Hard stop. Next passage. |
| Task 1 | Write the overview, then two body paragraphs. |
| Task 2 | Answer the question that was actually asked. |
| Speaking | Answer, reason, example. Then stop talking. |

---

## The night before

Read this page and your error card. Nothing else.
Bag: ID (**the same document you booked with**), booking confirmation, water.

**Sleep.** Tiredness destroys accuracy first, and accuracy is where band 7 lives.

---

## One bad section does not decide your band

The average of four does. A 5.5 in one paper survives if the other three hold.
Finish every paper. Never leave a box empty.
