# Appendix D — Pre-test-day checklist

Actionable lines only. Tick them.

---

## The week before

### Confirm with your test centre — these are the ones this book could not settle

Every item here was researched against official sources and could **not** be
confirmed. One phone call answers all five.

- [ ] **Delivery mode.** Confirm computer-delivered, not IELTS Online. They are
      different products. *IELTS Online is not accepted for immigration purposes,
      and institutional acceptance is optional* — at ~TND 750 a sitting, booking
      the wrong one is an expensive discovery.
- [ ] **Keyboard layout — AZERTY or QWERTY?** Published nowhere. If you train on
      one and sit the other, every minute in your Writing timing plan is
      optimistic. Ask, then practise on that layout.
- [ ] **Speaking: same day or a different day?** For computer-delivered both
      partners point to same day, but the published scheduling windows disagree
      with each other. **Trust your booking confirmation over any website.**
- [ ] **One Skill Retake — is it available at your centre?** Offered in 110+
      countries, but no official page names a Tunisian centre. Do not treat it as
      your safety net until someone confirms it.
- [ ] **Pen or pencil?** The British Council's policy page bans personal
      stationery; Cambridge's FAQ says you may write in pen or pencil. Follow
      whatever your centre tells you on the day.

### Admin

- [ ] ID booked with = ID you will carry. **The same document**, not an
      equivalent one.
- [ ] Booking confirmation saved offline — screenshot it, don't rely on signal.
- [ ] Test centre address checked, and the journey timed at the same hour of day.
- [ ] Fee paid and receipt kept (TND, Academic ~750).

### Preparation

- [ ] At least one **full timed** Listening + Reading sitting completed
      back-to-back, no pausing, typed answers, 2-minute check only.
- [ ] At least one **full 60-minute** Writing mock, both tasks, typed, on your
      test keyboard layout.
- [ ] Your last three `/review` sessions read again — the recurring patterns, not
      the individual errors.
- [ ] `handbook.md` regenerated and read once.
- [ ] Recorded yourself on a Part 2 and listened back for the three named
      features: **speech rate, stress-timing, chunking**.

### The night before

- [ ] No new material. Nothing you learn tonight will be retrievable tomorrow.
- [ ] Reread only: this appendix, `CHEAT-SHEETS.md`, and your own error card
      (Appendix C).
- [ ] Bag packed: ID, booking confirmation, water, layers.
- [ ] Alarm set with a backup.
- [ ] **Sleep.** Fatigue destroys Writing accuracy first, and accuracy is where
      band 7 is decided.

## Test day

### Arrival

- [ ] Arrive early. No official page publishes an arrival time — assume 30
      minutes and confirm with your centre.
- [ ] Expect ID check, photograph, and biometrics.
- [ ] Phone off and stowed. A phone that sounds can void your test.

### Listening — ~30 minutes

- [ ] Use every second of reading time to **predict**: type of word, singular or
      plural, and two or three candidate answers per gap.
- [ ] Type as you listen. 🖥️ **No transfer time. 2 minutes to check.**
- [ ] **One answer per gap.** Two scores zero even when one is right.
- [ ] Answers come in order — for every question type. Miss one, move forward.
      Hunting backwards turns one lost mark into three.
- [ ] Never a blank. A guess costs nothing.
- [ ] The 2 minutes are for **spelling and plurals only** — not for
      reconsidering, which usually makes answers worse.

### Reading — 60 minutes, no transfer time

- [ ] Hard stops: about 20 minutes per passage. Obey them even mid-question.
- [ ] Matching Headings early. Matching Information last.
- [ ] 🖥️ **Copy-paste from the passage** for completion answers — it makes
      spelling errors structurally impossible. **CTRL+F does not work.**
- [ ] True/False/Not Given: *can I point at the sentence that contradicts this?*
      If not → **NOT GIVEN**. Do not reflex-guess it; decide from what you read.
- [ ] Never a blank.

### Writing — 60 minutes

- [ ] **Task 2 first if you tend to run out of time.** It is worth double.
- [ ] Task 1: stop writing at **17 minutes**, check to 20.
- [ ] Task 2: 5 minutes planning, stop writing at **37 minutes**, check to 40.
- [ ] Underline **every part** of the Task 2 question before planning. A
      half-answered question is an automatic band 6 on Task Response.
- [ ] Task 1: write the **overview** first if you must. Without it, Task
      Achievement is capped.
- [ ] Paraphrase the prompt. **Copied rubric is discounted before your words are
      counted.**
- [ ] Check the on-screen word count. Do not estimate it.
- [ ] Final 3 minutes: proofread for **your** errors, in this order —
      **articles** (and the counter-list), **uncountables** (*informations*,
      *researches*), **third-person -s**, **run-ons**.
- [ ] Delete and repair. **Never add** in the final pass.

### Speaking — 11–14 minutes

- [ ] Warm your voice up: ten minutes of English aloud beforehand.
- [ ] Part 1: two or three sentences per answer. Answer + reason + example.
- [ ] Part 2: the prep minute is for **keywords, not sentences**. Reading aloud
      from notes flattens delivery and costs Fluency and Pronunciation.
- [ ] Speak the full two minutes. Being stopped **means you spoke long enough**.
- [ ] Part 3: expect pushback. Concede what is fair, then hold your ground with a
      reason. That is what is being tested.
- [ ] Don't know a word? **Paraphrase.** That is a band-7 move, not a failure.
- [ ] Slow down. A rapid speech rate is named at band 6 as damaging your rhythm.

### When you get stuck

One rule per paper. Use it and move.

| Paper | The rule |
|---|---|
| Listening | Guess and move forward. Never chase backwards. |
| Reading | Hard stop. Next passage. Come back if time allows. |
| Writing T1 | Write the overview, then the two body paragraphs. |
| Writing T2 | Answer the question that was actually asked. |
| Speaking | Answer, reason, example. Then stop. |

**One bad section does not decide your band.** The average of four does. A 5.5 in
one paper survives if the other three hold.

## Afterwards

- [ ] Results: plan against **5 days** (roughly half arrive within 1 day, 80%
      within 2, but official pages range up to 5).
- [ ] If one skill falls short, ask about **One Skill Retake** — availability at
      your centre still unconfirmed.
- [ ] **Enquiry on Results: apply within six weeks** of the test date. Fee
      refunded if a band changes. Only IDP states a score cannot fall; the other
      partners are silent — check your centre's terms before assuming a re-mark
      is risk-free.
- [ ] Scores are valid for two years.
