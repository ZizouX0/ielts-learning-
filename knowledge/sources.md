## 1. Current IELTS Academic format and timings — VERIFIED

| Paper | Verified detail |
|---|---|
| Total test time | 2 hours 45 minutes |
| Listening | ~30 minutes, 4 parts, 10 questions per part, 40 total |
| Reading (Academic) | 60 minutes, 3 passages, 40 questions, 2,150–2,750 words total, **no extra transfer time** |
| Writing | 60 minutes, 2 tasks (Task 1 ≥150 words / ~20 min; Task 2 ≥250 words / ~40 min) |
| Speaking | 11–14 minutes, 3 parts (P1 4–5 min, P2 3–4 min incl. 1 min prep, P3 4–5 min) |

Sources:
- <https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test>
- <https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-listening>
- <https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-reading>
- <https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing>
- <https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-speaking>

### Paper vs computer — a real difference that changes how you train

- **On paper:** ~30 min Listening **plus 10 minutes** to transfer answers to the
  answer sheet.
- **On computer:** ~30 min Listening, **no transfer time**, only **2 minutes** to
  check answers at the end. You type answers directly as you listen.
- Reading is 60 minutes with no extra transfer time in **both** modes.

Source: <https://ielts.idp.com/prepare/article-ielts-on-computer-vs-ielts-on-paper>

## 3. Raw score to band conversion — VERIFIED

Approximate marks out of 40 needed, per official guidance:

| Band | Listening | Academic Reading |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | 16 | 15 |
| 6 | 23 | 23 |
| 7 | 30 | 30 |
| 8 | 35 | 35 |

Official caveat: conversion **varies slightly between test versions** — these are
averages, not a guarantee. Treat them as planning targets, not promises.

Overall band = mean of the four skill bands, rounded to the nearest half band.
An average ending in .25 rounds up to the next half band; .75 rounds up to the
next whole band. Worked example from the official source: 6.5 / 6.5 / 5.0 / 7.0
averages 6.25 and is reported as **6.5**.

Within Writing, **Task 2 counts twice as much as Task 1**.

Sources:
- <https://www.ielts.org/organisations/ielts-for-organisations/understanding-ielts-scoring>
- <https://ielts.org/for-test-takers/how-ielts-is-scored>

## 5. Recent changes — VERIFIED, and this one affects you

**IELTS is discontinuing the paper-based test.** From mid-2026 IELTS will no
longer be offered on paper; all tests move to computer delivery, with exact
timelines varying by market. Also confirmed:

- Results already obtained on paper stay valid for the usual two-year period.
- A **"Writing on Paper"** option is being introduced in selected markets: you
  sit the test on computer but handwrite the Writing responses.
- The change **does not alter** the skills assessed, the test construct, or how
  results are interpreted. Score comparability across delivery modes is
  research-backed.
- Access arrangements (large print, braille, hearing support) are unchanged.
- One Skill Retake now extends to Writing on Paper, and must match the delivery
  mode of the original test.

Sources:
- <https://ielts.org/news-and-insights/updates-to-ielts-test-delivery>
- <https://ielts.idp.com/about/news-and-articles/article-updates-to-ielts-test-delivery>

**Practical consequence for training:** assume computer-delivered unless your
booking says otherwise. Train Listening with **no transfer time** and typing
directly, and practise Writing by typing — your real typing speed and on-screen
proofreading become part of your score.

### One Skill Retake in Tunisia — NOT CONFIRMED

One Skill Retake is available in **over 110 countries**, computer-delivered only,
at selected centres; it is not available in the United States. Official sources
publish no country-by-country list, so **Tunisia could not be confirmed either
way** from official material.

Do not treat OSR as your safety net until you have confirmed it directly with
your own test centre. Verify via the official centre finder or by contacting the
British Council or IDP centre in Tunis when you book.

Sources:
- <https://ielts.org/take-a-test/booking-your-test/one-skill-retake>
- <https://ielts.org/organisations/ielts-for-organisations/verifying-ielts-results/one-skill-retake>
