# Listening drill (text-adapted)

**The constraint, stated honestly.** This is a text environment — I cannot play
you audio. What follows trains the four things that actually cost marks and can
be trained on the page: **prediction, trap-recognition, spelling, and transfer
discipline**. Real audio practice is homework — official free tests are linked in
`README.md`, and you should do one per week alongside these drills.

## Before you start

Read `progress/error-log.md` for my logged Listening errors. Pick whichever of
the four drills below targets my weakest area. If there is no data, run Drill A
then Drill B.

Remember 🖥️: computer-delivered IELTS gives **no transfer time** — 2 minutes to
check at the end. Everything here assumes typing answers live.

---

## Drill A — Prediction

Give me a set of **10 gapped items** from a plausible Listening task (form
completion, note completion, table, or sentence completion), with the surrounding
text but **no audio and no answers**.

Ask me, for each gap, to state:
1. **Type** — number / date / time / name / place / noun / verb / adjective
2. **Singular or plural**
3. **Two or three words I would expect to hear**

Then mark my predictions. Show which gaps were unambiguously constrained by
grammar and which were genuinely open. Explain how a correct prediction narrows
what I have to listen for from *everything* to *one thing*.

## Drill B — Trap-spotting on transcripts

Write an **original** dialogue or monologue transcript (200–350 words) in
authentic IELTS style — a student and a tutor, a booking enquiry, a tour guide,
a seminar discussion. Never reproduce a real test.

Plant, and later name, these traps:

- **The correction trap** — a value stated then revised (*sorry, that's the
  monthly figure — the deposit is actually...*). The answer is what follows the
  correction.
- **Rejected-option distractors** — all three MCQ options mentioned, two rejected
  (*we did consider the Thursday, but...*).
- **The plural trap** — a countable noun that must be plural in the answer.
- **Word-limit bait** — a natural answer that runs to four words, where the limit
  is two.
- **The paraphrase** — the question's words never appear; the idea does.

Give me the questions, let me answer from the transcript, then mark and
**name each trap by type** so I learn the category, not the instance.

## Drill C — Spelling dictation

Take 15 words from the recurring-misspellings list in `knowledge/tricks.md`, plus
any word I have previously spelled wrong in `progress/error-log.md`.

Give each as a **definition or a gapped sentence**, never the word itself. I type
the spelling. Mark strictly — a misspelled answer scores zero on test day, and
this is the cheapest band you will ever recover.

Focus on: double letters (*accommodation*, *committee*, *necessary*), *-tion* vs
*-sion*, silent letters (*receipt*, *foreign*), and the
thirteen/thirty–fourteen/forty–fifteen/fifty stress pairs.

## Drill D — Transfer and answer-order discipline

Present a **10-question sequence with a deliberate disruption**: the material
answers question 6 before question 5, or skips 7 entirely.

Ask me what I would do in real time. Then teach the rule explicitly:

> Answers come in order, always. If you hear the answer to Q8 while still hunting
> for Q6, Q6 and Q7 are gone. Type a plausible guess for them **immediately** and
> lock on to Q8. Chasing a missed answer backwards costs you the next three.

Also drill:
- Never leaving a blank (no negative marking)
- Obeying the word limit exactly
- 🖥️ Using the 2 minutes at the end for spelling and plurals only — not for
  reconsidering answers, which usually makes them worse

---

## After any drill

Mark strictly. For every error, state which rule was broken — prediction,
correction trap, distractor, spelling, plural, word limit, or answer order.

Append to `progress/error-log.md` and `progress/band-tracker.md`, plus any new
vocabulary to `progress/vocab-bank.md`. Commit and push:
`progress: listening — <drill> — x/10`

## Homework

Assign **one official free Listening test** before the next session (links in
`README.md`), done under real conditions: no pausing, no replaying, typed
answers, 2-minute check. Tell me to report my raw score and which question
numbers I missed so we can log the pattern.

## End the session

One line: the single highest-impact fix.
